Many small agents. One clear picture.

Everything needed to make something look and sound like Loose Ends: the logo, the colours, the type, the parts and the voice. The feel is precise and calm, with one warm signal.

Every file is self-contained: the wordmark is converted to outlines, so no font needs to be installed. Space Grotesk is used under the SIL Open Font License.

  • Clear space: keep the width of one outer dot free on every side.
  • Smallest size: 16px for the mark, 20px tall for the full logo.
  • Motion: the dots may gather into the mark once, or turn one step on hover. The mark never loops.
  • Do not recolour the centre dot, or colour any outer dot amber.
  • Do not stretch, outline, tilt or add a shadow or glow to the mark.
  • Do not set the name in capitals or in another typeface.

Buttons

Points down the pageRead on
  • Shape: 10px radius (8px small), Inter 600 at 15px (14px small), 22px side padding, an arrow that shows where the button goes.
  • Hover: the primary brightens, lifts one pixel and casts a soft amber shadow. The secondary fills with Ink 800 and its border lightens. The arrow moves three pixels.
  • Pressed: both drop one pixel and darken.
  • Keyboard focus: a 2px ring, 3px off the edge. Amber on the secondary, paper on the primary.
  • Reduced motion: colours still change; nothing moves.

Cards and status

A card is Ink 800 with a one-pixel line and a 10px radius. Status is a mono label in capitals. Teal means verified or built, red means rejected, amber means active now, grey means not yet.

Dot field and motion

The motif is a field of small dots: scattered when nothing is known, in a grid once it is mapped. Each dot is one agent or one record.

Scattered · before
Grid · mapped
Gathered · the mark
  • Ground: 1.2px dots on a 26px grid at low contrast, fading out toward the text. It sits behind every second section.
  • One amber dot at most in any small group. Most dots are slate.
  • Motion means work: dots move when the picture shows agents reading, a finding being checked, or the mark gathering. Nothing moves for decoration alone.
  • Motion is honest: an animation is labelled an illustration unless it shows real data. It draws only what the product does.
  • Reduced motion: every animation has a finished still frame, and that frame is what loads first.

Voice

Found, then proven. Plain words, short sentences, and nothing we cannot point to.

Say

Pull request 41 in dispatch-api is open with no reviewer assigned.

Not

We believe AI can unlock transformative synergies across your engineering organisation.

  • Lead with what was found, then the evidence for it.
  • Say we found, never we believe. If it is not verified, say unverified or leave it out.
  • A number always carries its source and its period.
  • Say what is not built, not examined or not in place. Planned means not built.
  • Report queues, teams and handoffs. Never judge a person.
  • No "leverage", "synergy", "unlock" or "AI magic". No bees or honey.
