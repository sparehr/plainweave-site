What you get

  • The Plainweave Audit report. Findings about ownership, handoffs and repeated manual work, each linked to the records it rests on. The full sample report shows what one looks like, for an invented company.
  • A list of what was and was not examined, so a quiet area reads as unexamined, not as fine.
  • Every finding read by a person against its evidence before you see it.
  • A walkthrough call to go through the report with you.
  • Deletion 30 days after the call. Ask before then and you get a copy of what we held.

What we ask

  • One person who can approve read-only access for the company: a founder, COO or head of operations.
  • Your admin connects the apps. One approval each for Slack and Google Workspace, and GitHub if you use it.
  • The walkthrough call, and honest answers to a fixed set of questions: what you would act on, what is wrong, what is missing, and whether you would pay for it.
  • Optionally, permission to describe the engagement without naming you. You can say no.
  • Nothing else. No fee, no software to install, no developer time.

A good fit

  • Your teams work in Slack and Google Workspace. GitHub is a plus.
  • Work crosses teams: support to operations, sales to delivery, code to review.
  • One person can approve access for the whole company.
  • Some work runs on paper or an older system. A plus, not a need: we see it through a walkthrough, a CSV export and photos of blank forms, with no access to those systems.

Not a fit yet

  • Work that lives in other tools. Plainweave has connectors for Jira, HubSpot, Zendesk and others, but for a real company only Slack, Google Workspace and GitHub can be connected today.
  • Needing message or file content read. The design-partner audit is metadata only, with no exceptions.

How it runs

About two weeks from signed scope to the walkthrough call.

A first conversation

We check the fit and go through the security page with whoever will approve access.

Scope in writing

The exact sources and resources, the time window, the questions you care about, what will not be examined, and the deletion date. You sign a data processing agreement before any access.

Your admin connects the apps

Read-only and metadata only: names, counts, timestamps and owners. Never message text or file content. You see every permission and why it is needed before your admin approves.

We run the audit and read every finding

Roles map each area, a verifier rejects anything it cannot trace to its source, and a person reviews what is left against the evidence.

The walkthrough

You open the signed report and we go through it together. Then the fixed questions.

Disconnect and delete

Your admin removes the apps. We delete your data 30 days after the call and tell you what the deletion receipt covers.

Before we start

Said up front, because it changes when the audit can happen.

  • Plainweave has not run on a real company yet. Before the first outside audit, it runs once in an internal pilot. We are lining up the design partner now, so the audit can start right after.
  • The apps are not registered yet. The Slack, Google and GitHub apps your admin will approve, and the hosting your report is served from, come before your audit.
  • The data processing agreement is a draft to come. You get it before you approve anything.

Ask about being the design partner

Tell us what your company does, roughly how many people work there, and which tools your teams use.
